Is investing for you?

Investing means putting money into assets that may grow in value or pay income. Unlike saving money in cash, the value can move down as well as up. Only invest money you can leave untouched for a while.

It may suit you if…

  • You have money left after essentials.
  • You can leave it invested for years.
  • You accept that values can fall.
  • You are prepared to learn first.

Pause first if…

  • You need the money soon for rent or tuition.
  • You have expensive debt to handle first.
  • You expect guaranteed or quick profit.
  • You are acting mainly because of hype.

Five words worth knowing

Share

A small ownership piece of one company.

ETF

A basket of investments bought as one product.

Return

The gain or loss on your money.

Risk

The chance your value falls or a goal is missed.

Diversification

Spreading money across investments.

Before your first investment

  1. Protect your basics. Keep an emergency buffer and avoid investing money you need soon.
  2. Understand costs and taxes. Fees, FX, TOB, and dividend taxes affect results.
  3. Start simple. Learn the difference between real shares/ETFs and higher-risk CFDs.
